# Franchise HQ v5.5.3a

## Depth Chart & Schedule Interaction Hotfix

This hotfix improves the Team Depth Chart and Schedule interactions without changing roster data.

### Included

- Development-trait colors on all depth-chart cards, including backups
- X-Factor red, Superstar gold, Star silver, Normal default
- Larger starter and backup cards with more readable names
- Wide receivers moved below the offensive-line row
- Rebalanced defensive front and linebacker alignment
- Schedule filters preserve the current scroll position

### Non-goals

- No roster data changes
- No depth-order editing
- No schedule editing
- No statistics changes
- No player-card redesign
