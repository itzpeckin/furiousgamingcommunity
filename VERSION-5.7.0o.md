# Franchise HQ v5.7.0o

## Trade Center Stabilization & Reconciliation

This release consolidates the Phase 5.7 QA findings into one new baseline. It adds Committee queue filters, a persistent multi-draft workflow, package-engine commissioner controls, multi-team action fixes, AI suggestion fixes, approval-time asset reconciliation, approved-trade cancellation, and clearer trade terminology.

Approved trades remain administrative records only. Franchise HQ does not mutate Madden-authoritative rosters, depth charts, cap tables, or draft-pick ownership.
