# Franchise HQ v5.7.3a

## Notification Read-State & Analytics Drilldown Hotfix

This hotfix changes notification read behavior so opening or closing the notification bell does not mark any notification read. A notification becomes read only when the user selects that specific notification. Opening the related trade through another Trade Center tab does not clear the notification.

The Analytics page now makes completed-trade totals clickable. League totals, the signed-in team total, and each team count can open an inline list of the approved trades represented by that number. The active season filter is respected.

No trade workflow, roster, Trade Block, valuation, or Madden-authority behavior was changed.
