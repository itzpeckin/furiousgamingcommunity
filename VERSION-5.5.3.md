# Franchise HQ v5.5.3 — Team Experience Expansion

## Included

- Depth Chart selection keeps the current scroll position.
- Depth Chart player tiles are larger and use clearer selected and unselected treatments.
- Team Stats displays position-specific tables for Passing, Rushing, Receiving, Defense, Kicking, and Punting.
- Team Schedule supports Preseason and Regular Season filters.
- The Regular Season view contains the full season schedule with completed results and upcoming games.
- The Playoffs filter and playoff schedule remain hidden until the active league phase reaches the postseason.

## Boundaries

This release remains read-only. It does not edit roster assignments, player statistics, game results, contracts, schedules, or Madden snapshots.
