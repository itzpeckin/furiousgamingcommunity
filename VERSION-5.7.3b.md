# Franchise HQ v5.7.3b

## Analytics Modal & Notification Panel Controls Hotfix

### Changes

- Completed-trade analytics drilldowns now open in a centered modal card.
- The modal contains its own Season filter, defaulted to All Seasons each time it opens.
- Clicking the backdrop closes the analytics modal.
- Clicking the notification bell or opening the panel does not mark notifications read.
- Clicking outside the notification panel closes it.
- Added Mark all read so users may intentionally clear the unread notification count without opening every trade.
- Clicking an individual notification still marks only that notification read and routes to its trade.
