# Franchise HQ v5.8.2c — Leader Alignment & Readability Patch

This patch aligns the top of the League Leader tables directly beneath the featured matchup and improves leaderboard readability by reallocating horizontal space from the rank and statistic columns to player names.

## Changes

- Removed the extra Stat Leaders section-heading gap on desktop.
- Positioned all four leader tables immediately beneath the matchup panel.
- Preserved bottom alignment with the standings column.
- Narrowed the rank column.
- Narrowed and reduced the font size of the selected-statistic column.
- Increased player-name font size and usable width.
- Tightened secondary position/team metadata.
- Reduced leaderboard header and selector sizing slightly.
