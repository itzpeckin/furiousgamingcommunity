# Franchise HQ v5.7.3

## Trade Notifications & Completed Trade Analytics

This release completes the Trade Center Version 1.0 roadmap with two focused capabilities:

- Trade workflow notifications delivered through the existing notification bell.
- Lightweight completed-trade analytics by season and franchise.

### Notifications

Notifications now cover the important trade lifecycle events already supported by Franchise HQ, including new offers, counters and revisions, participant acceptances, committee-review assignments, final approval or rejection, automatic invalidation, withdrawal, and Commissioner cancellation.

Opening a notification routes to the related trade and marks it read. Opening the related trade directly also clears matching unread notifications. A Mark all read action is available from the notification menu.

Committee-review notifications are sent only to eligible, non-recused reviewers.

### Analytics

A new Analytics tab appears after History.

All users can view:

- Total completed league trades.
- Their franchise's completed-trade count.
- A season filter.

Commissioners can also view completed trades by every team.

Only approved trades count. A multi-team transaction counts once for the league and once for each participating franchise. Commissioner-cancelled trades do not count as completed.

### Data authority

This release does not modify rosters, depth charts, cap tables, draft-pick ownership, or Madden snapshot data. Madden Companion remains the authoritative source for roster movement.
