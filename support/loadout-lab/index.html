Weekly loadout checks belong in Discord

The experimental web test tool has been retired. You do not need to upload screenshots here, crop images or label abilities.

When your commissioner enables weekly loadout checks, post your screenshot in your league’s current scheduling thread. The Discord bot reads it automatically and replies there. Use /loadout status or /loadout missing in Discord to check progress.

Commissioners can continue managing banned abilities and duplicate-ability rules in FHQ League Controls.
