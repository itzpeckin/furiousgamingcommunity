# Franchise HQ v5.7.0d

## Multi-Team Active Trade & Fairness Hotfix

This hotfix makes submitted three-team and four-team trades first-class Trade Center negotiations.

### Included

- Multi-team trades appear in Active, Incoming, Committee, and History views according to status and visibility.
- Multi-team trade detail includes private participant chat.
- Trade creator can modify or withdraw a negotiating multi-team trade.
- Revisions retain the same transaction ID and reset participant acceptance.
- Multi-team Fair Trade Calculator compares each team's outgoing and incoming value.
- Asset-level valuation rows open the existing player or draft-pick valuation card.
- Approved or submitted trades remain read-only with respect to roster, cap, depth-chart, and League Data ownership.
