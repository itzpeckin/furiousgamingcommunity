# Franchise HQ v5.7.2a — Completed Trade History & Search Consistency

- History is restricted to committee-approved completed trades.
- History uses the same trade cards as Sent, Received, Committee, Approved, and Rejected.

- Date filters were removed.
- Team Trade History shows approved trades only.
