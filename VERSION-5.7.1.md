# Franchise HQ v5.7.1 — Trade Block & Needs-Based AI Suggestions

This release redesigns the Trade Block as the authoritative marketplace for AI Suggestions. Owners list available players and picks, select multiple position and draft-pick needs, and receive suggestions only when both teams have mutually compatible listed assets.

## Included
- League Trade Block and My Trade Block views
- Player and pick listings
- Position-needs multi-select
- Draft-pick-needs multi-select
- League-wide asset filters
- Trade Block-only AI asset pool
- Mutual-needs matching
- Needs Match score
- Optional Trade Balance score when the calculator is enabled
- Clickable player and pick assets
- Build This Trade integration
- Trade-limit and approved-asset awareness
