# Franchise HQ v5.7.0 — Trade Asset Resolution & Multi-Team Trade Foundation

## Included
- Authoritative trade asset resolver backed by the active League Data and Roster services.
- Player and draft-pick ownership verification.
- Duplicate and invalid directed-transfer detection.
- Working three-team and four-team trade builder.
- Maximum of four participating franchises.
- Every asset has an explicit sending team and receiving team.
- Every participating team must both send and receive at least one asset.
- Unanimous owner acceptance before committee review.
- Existing two-team trade workflows remain available.
