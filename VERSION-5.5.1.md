# Franchise HQ v5.5.1 — Roster Experience

## Scope

This release combines the originally planned Team Roster Page and Player Directory/Search releases into one read-only roster experience.

## Delivered

- Team roster pages powered by `FranchiseHQ.modules.league.rosters`
- Offense, defense, special teams, injured reserve, practice squad, and other groups
- Depth-order presentation
- Roster summary and health information
- Contract, cap, age, rating, development trait, injury, and roster-status display
- Snapshot/import provenance
- League-wide player directory
- Search and combined filters for team, position, roster status, development trait, OVR, and age
- Free-agent/unassigned view
- Source-aware player detail modal
- Empty and Development mode compatibility

## Boundaries

The experience remains read-only. This release does not edit rosters, contracts, injuries, depth charts, or Madden snapshots.
