# Franchise HQ v5.7.0w

## Trade Settings Consolidation & Trade Center Cleanup

This release consolidates player-value weights with their underlying curves, adds Contract & Control settings, simplifies team draft-pick projections into four ranges, removes Trade Center and Trade Draft visual clutter, resets participant position filters correctly, simplifies sent-trade labels, protects unsaved builder navigation, and limits Committee sub-filters to authorized reviewers.

### Elite Premium

Elite Premium is a player-level boost applied after the normal OVR, development, age, position, contract, and production components. It can overlap with the OVR 93+ bonus and Package Engine best-player or elite-scarcity adjustments. Commissioners may set Elite Premium to 0% to disable this player-level boost while retaining the other elite-player protections.
