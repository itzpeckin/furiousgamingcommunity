# Franchise HQ v5.7.1a

## Trade Block Controls & Team Needs Cleanup

This hotfix removes duplicate Trade Block management controls, simplifies team needs to position-based needs, and improves spacing between team names and owners in Trade Block and AI Suggestion cards.

### Changed

- Kept one `Manage My Trade Block` action on the Trade Block page.
- Removed the duplicate `Manage assets & needs` button.
- Removed Draft Pick Needs from the Trade Block manager.
- Renamed Position Needs to Team Needs.
- AI readiness now reports listed assets and team needs only.
- Needs matching now uses selected position needs only.
- Improved team-name and owner spacing in AI Suggestion cards.

### Unchanged

- Owners can still place players and draft picks on the Trade Block.
- Draft picks remain available as listed trade assets.
- Trade limits, asset availability, Fair Trade Calculator behavior, and Build This Trade remain unchanged.
