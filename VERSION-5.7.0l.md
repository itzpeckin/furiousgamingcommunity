# Franchise HQ v5.7.0l

## Committee Authorization & Phase 5.7 QA

This hotfix makes the Trade Committee roster authoritative from Commissioner HQ → Teams & Owners, removes committee ballot controls from non-reviewers, correctly recuses committee members whose franchise participates in a trade, and standardizes two-team and multi-team committee rosters into a two-column layout.

It also adds a Commissioner-only Reset All Trade Data utility so Phase 5.7 can be validated from a clean state without changing trade rules, valuation settings, rosters, or League Data.
