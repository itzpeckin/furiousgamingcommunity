# Franchise HQ v5.7.0g

## Approved Trade Credit Enforcement & Commissioner Usage

- Seasonal trade credits now count only approved Trade Committee decisions.
- Active negotiations and committee-review trades no longer reserve credits.
- Team owners with zero approved credits remaining cannot start a new private trade.
- Trade-partner options show team, owner, and credits remaining; teams at zero are disabled.
- Teams & Owners includes a clickable Trades Remaining column and approved-trade history.
- Builder submission displays a clear notification instead of silently doing nothing.
- Final committee approval is blocked if another approved trade has exhausted a team's remaining credits.
- Season Trade Usage cosmetics were corrected to prevent overlapping panels.
