# Franchise HQ v5.6.0b

## Confidence Pool Controls & Predictive Auto-Picks

This hotfix expands the v5.6.0 Confidence Pool with season/week clearing controls and a history-based predictive auto-pick model.

### Included

- Clear all picks for the selected week.
- Clear the entire season entry with typed confirmation.
- Auto-Pick & Assign Week based only on owner game history.
- Neutral 50-point baseline for every owner before completed games exist.
- Gradual rating movement as completed results accumulate.
- No home-field advantage.
- No Madden team ratings or roster ratings.
- Hidden owner-strength calculation.
- Commissioner-only team-owner rank inside Commissioner HQ → Teams & Owners.
- Existing manual selections are preserved when auto-assignment is run.
- Confidence values are ranked from the closest matchup to the strongest predicted advantage.

### Historical factors

- Career win percentage: 30%
- Total wins: 15%
- Playoff wins: 15%
- Super Bowl appearances: 10%
- Super Bowl wins: 15%
- Point differential per game: 15%

A reliability adjustment keeps every new owner near the 50 baseline until enough games have been completed.
