# Franchise HQ v5.7.0x — Final Trade Center Controls & Calculator Toggle

This is the final planned v5.7.0 feature release before v5.7.1 Trade Block changes.

## Included
- Removed backend-oriented Section and Commissioner utility labels from Commissioner Controls.
- Changed Overall player-value settings to a five-column row.
- Removed duplicate team abbreviations from Team Owner Pick Projections.
- Expanded Projected Range selectors.
- Removed Draft Pick Projections from League Rules.
- Added a Commissioner master switch to enable or disable the Trade Calculator across Franchise HQ.
