# Franchise HQ v5.9.1.2 — Companion Teams Importer

This release activates normalized Madden Companion team records through the protected import framework.

## Included
- Teams-only importer
- Candidate snapshot creation
- Modular validation before activation
- Automatic rejection on failure
- Import-history record
- `league:dataUpdated` publication
- Partial-dataset provenance showing players are still pending
- Commissioner HQ import control and result feedback

## Boundary
Player and roster records are not activated in this release. That work remains in v5.9.1.3.
