## FranchiseHQ: 2027 season quick start

We’re moving league tools and trades into **FranchiseHQ** for the 2027 season.

**Before you start:** use your league Discord account, join FHQ with `/join` if needed, and have a commissioner assign your team. Allow bot DMs. When choosing a `/command`, select **FranchiseHQ**—NeonSportz has similar commands.

**Quick lookups**
- `/player` — type a name and select a player.
- `/leaders` — choose a stat to see its top 10.
- `/gm-history all` or `player` — GM wins, playoff trips, Super Bowl appearances and titles.
- `/standings all`, `conference` or `division` — use **name** to select a specific group.
- `/playoffs` — top 10 teams per conference; `/eliminated` — teams out by record.
- `/games played` or `unplayed` — this week’s games. Results update after a Madden import.
- `/rules category` or `section` — select the rules you need.

**Trade block**
Star a player on your FHQ team page, or use `/trade-block add` or `remove`. Both update the same block. `/trade-block view` shows league listings; select **team** for just your team.

**How trades work**
1. Use `/trade create`, choose the other owner, and select what you send and receive. Add slots as needed—up to **6 players/picks per side**. For 3–4 teams, use `/trade multi-team` to open FHQ.
2. Negotiate in your **private thread**. **Counter Offer** opens FHQ to edit. A partner rejection triggers a DM and closes the thread.
3. **Accept Trade** sends the deal to **Trade-Submit** for committee review. Vote totals update in both places; reviewers can include a denial reason.
4. **Approved:** you get a DM, the thread closes, and you can send the trade in Madden. **Committee denial:** the thread stays open; **Revise Trade** opens FHQ to adjust and resubmit.

**Wait for approval before trading in Madden.** Missing a draft pick or need help? Contact a commissioner.
